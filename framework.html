The WEALTH™ Book Discovery System

A strategic framework for helping authors move from being published to being discoverable.

Six Stages

One Word, One Method.

WEALTH is a working checklist as much as a name. Each stage answers one question about your book, and each answer shapes the next.

W

WORKUP

Work up the book's current position and identify its discovery opportunities.

E

EXPLORE

Explore the readers, platforms and search behavior that matter for this book.

A

ALIGN

Align positioning, keywords, categories and product page with what readers look for.

L

LAUNCH

Launch the book in front of relevant audiences with focused promotion.

T

TURN

Turn discovery into readership and long term audience relationships.

H

HARVEST

Harvest what works, refine it and build sustainable marketing systems.

The goal isn't to promote everywhere.

It's to identify where your book has the greatest opportunity to be discovered, and build a strategy around it.
